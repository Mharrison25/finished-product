Patio cleaning in Carroll County, GA

Concrete patios in west Georgia turn green and black in the shade. We pressure wash patios and porch slabs for $0.15 per square foot.

Pricing
  • $0.15 per square foot

What it costs

Multiply length by width. A 15 by 20 foot patio is 300 square feet, or $45. A 20 by 20 foot patio is 400 square feet, or $60.

What we clean

Concrete patios, porch slabs, and steps. Algae, mildew, red clay, and grill grease come up with a surface cleaner, so the slab dries even instead of striped.

Add the driveway

Most customers do the patio and the driveway in one visit. It is the same rate, so add the square footage together.

Questions

Will it hurt the plants around my patio?

We rinse nearby plants before and after. Tell us about any beds right against the slab.

What about pavers or wood decks?

Send a photo and ask. We say yes only when we can do it without damaging the surface.
