Where we work in Carroll County, GA

We are based in Carrollton and work across Carroll County. Open Monday to Sunday. Here is each place we serve and what to know before you book.

Carrollton

Our home base and the county seat, with about 28,000 residents.

Villa Rica

About 11,800 residents live on the Carroll County side. The city also reaches into Douglas County, so send your address first.

Temple

A fast-growing city of about 6,300 in Carroll County that also reaches into Haralson County.

Bremen

About 7,700 residents, spread across the Carroll and Haralson county line.

Bowdon

A town of about 2,200 people with a downtown of antique shops.

Mount Zion

A small city of about 2,000 people, less than 20 miles from Alabama.

Fairfield Plantation

A residential community of about 4,900 people.

Whitesburg and Roopville

Two small towns of about 630 and 240 people. We serve both by appointment.
