Window cleaning in Carroll County, GA

We clean windows for homes across Carroll County and price every job by the pane. Ground floor panes are $4. Upper story panes are $5. There is no square footage to measure and no guessing at the total.

Pricing
  • $4 per pane on the ground floor
  • $5 per pane on upper stories

Get your total in three steps

Count the panes on the ground floor. Count the panes on upper stories. Text us both numbers and we send back a price. A house with 16 ground floor panes and 8 upstairs panes is $64 plus $40, or $104.

Pick a schedule

Many homes do one visit in late spring and one in fall. Others call once a year. Tell us what you want and we will book it.

Questions

What counts as a pane?

One piece of glass. Count every separate piece, on every floor.

Can I clean only some of my windows?

Yes. Tell us which ones and how many. Six upstairs panes are $30.
