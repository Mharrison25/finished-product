How to clean pollen off windows in Georgia

Pine pollen covers cars, porches, and glass across Georgia every spring, usually in March and April. Cleaning at the wrong time means doing the job twice.

Pricing
  • $4 per pane on the ground floor
  • $5 per pane on upper stories

Wait for the end of the season

If you clean windows in the middle of pollen season, a new yellow coat shows up within days. Plan the cleaning for after the heaviest weeks, usually late April or May.

Do not wipe it dry

Wiping dry pollen across glass can smear it and scratch the surface if grit is mixed in. Rinse the glass first, then wash.

Rain does not clean it

Rain turns pollen into a film that dries on the glass. Windows can look worse after a storm than before it.
