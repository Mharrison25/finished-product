House washing in Mount Zion, GA

Mount Zion sits on the western side of Carroll County, close to the Alabama line. Many houses here sit under tall pines, which drop needles and sap on the siding and roof edges.

Pine needles and sap

Pine trees leave a sticky film and dark spots on siding. A soft wash lifts most of it, and we tell you if any sap spots need extra work.
