How much does window cleaning cost in Carroll County?

We charge by the pane. Ground floor panes are $4 each and panes on upper stories are $5 each. That is the whole rate.

Pricing
  • $4 per pane on the ground floor
  • $5 per pane on upper stories

Three examples

A small house with 12 ground floor panes is $48. A two-story house with 16 ground floor panes and 4 upstairs panes is $84. A larger house with 22 ground floor panes and 8 upstairs panes is $128.

How to count your panes

Walk around the house and count every separate piece of glass. Count ground floor and upper floors separately. Send us both numbers.

Anything that changes the quote

Tell us if a window is hard to reach, if a home has unusual glass, or if you want only part of the house done. We tell you whether it changes the price.
