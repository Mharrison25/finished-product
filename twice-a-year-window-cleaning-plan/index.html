A twice-a-year window cleaning schedule

Many homes in west Georgia do well with two window visits a year: one in late spring and one in fall. Each visit is priced by the pane, the same way as a single visit.

Pricing
  • $4 per pane on the ground floor
  • $5 per pane on upper stories

Why spring and fall

Late spring comes after the heaviest pollen. Fall comes after summer dust and before cold weather. Two visits keep windows clear through most of the year.

What it costs

A house with 16 ground floor panes and 4 upstairs panes is $84 per visit. Two visits a year come to $168.

How to set it up

Tell us you want a spring and a fall visit. We will book both and keep them on the calendar.
