How often should you clean your windows in Georgia?

Twice a year works for most homes: once after pollen season and once in fall. Your house may need more or fewer visits depending on its surroundings.

Pricing
  • $4 per pane on the ground floor
  • $5 per pane on upper stories

When twice is enough

A house with a few trees, a typical yard, and some porch cover usually stays clear with two visits.

When you may need more

Heavy tree cover drops pine needles, sap, and pollen on glass. Windows near a busy road collect dust and grime faster. Sprinklers that spray the glass leave spots, and new construction nearby adds dust.

When you may need fewer

Windows under a deep porch roof stay cleaner. Some of those homes do fine with one visit a year.

Count the cost

At $4 per ground floor pane and $5 upstairs, a house with 16 ground floor panes and 4 upstairs panes is $84 a visit. Twice a year is $168.
