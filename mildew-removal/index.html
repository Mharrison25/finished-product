Mildew and algae removal from siding in Carroll County, GA

Green and black streaks on siding are usually algae and mildew, and they grow fastest on shaded, north-facing walls. We remove them with a low-pressure soft wash.

Pricing
  • Priced per house from photos

Why it keeps coming back

West Georgia is warm and humid, and trees keep some walls in shade most of the day. Algae and mildew grow there first. A wash every one to two years keeps it under control.

How we remove it

We apply cleaner at low pressure, let it work, and rinse it away. That lifts the growth without forcing water behind vinyl or stripping paint.

Questions

Will it come back?

Eventually, in shaded spots. Cutting back branches that touch the house helps it stay clean longer.

Is it safe for paint and vinyl?

Yes. Soft washing uses low pressure, so paint and vinyl are not stripped or dented.
