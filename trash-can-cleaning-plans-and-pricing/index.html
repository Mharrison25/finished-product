Trash can cleaning cost and scheduling

Cleaning a trash can costs $50. The price is per can, so the total is easy to work out.

Cost by number of cans

One can is $50. Two cans are $100. Three cans are $150. Four cans are $200.

When to clean

Warm months are when cans smell worst. A can that holds food waste needs more attention than one that holds dry trash. If the smell lingers after pickup, book a visit.

Repeat visits and multiple units

Landlords with several units can book every can at a property in one visit. If you want cans cleaned on a repeat schedule, ask us and we will work out the timing.
