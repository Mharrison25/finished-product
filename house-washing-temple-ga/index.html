House washing in Temple, GA

Temple has grown quickly, and many of its houses are on lots with young trees and open yards. The siding gets full sun on some walls and stays damp on others, so we look at each side before pricing.

Why one side of the house gets dirtier

Walls that face away from the afternoon sun stay damp longer, and algae grows there first. Photos of each wall let us price the job accurately.

Siding, gutters, and trim

We wash the siding and the trim. Black streaks that run down the wall under a gutter corner usually wash off in one pass.
