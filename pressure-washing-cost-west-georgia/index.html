How much does pressure washing cost in west Georgia?

Our rate is $0.15 per square foot. The number is simple enough that you can work out your own price before you call.

Four examples

A 20 by 30 foot driveway is 600 square feet, or $90. A 12 by 40 foot driveway is 480 square feet, or $72. A 15 by 20 foot patio is 300 square feet, or $45. A 3 by 40 foot walkway is 120 square feet, or $18.

How to measure

Measure length and width in feet and multiply. For odd shapes, split the area into rectangles and add them. Photos help us check your math.

Comparing quotes

When you compare quotes, ask what the number covers. A per square foot price lets you check the math yourself, and it tells you what each surface costs.
