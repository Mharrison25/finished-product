Sidewalk and walkway cleaning in Carroll County, GA

Front walks and sidewalks show gray foot traffic and algae first. We pressure wash them for $0.15 per square foot.

Pricing
  • $0.15 per square foot

What it costs

A 4 by 50 foot walkway is 200 square feet, or $30. A 4 by 25 foot front walk is 100 square feet, or $15.

Entry walks, side paths, and steps

We clean front walks, side paths to the backyard, and concrete steps. Steps are measured like a flat surface: width times depth for each step, added up.

Best done with the driveway

Walks and driveways get dirty together. Doing both in one visit keeps the concrete the same color.

Questions

Can you get rid of black streaks?

Most algae and dirt streaks come off. Rust and old oil can stay faintly visible. We tell you what to expect before we start.

Do I need to be home?

No, as long as we can reach an outdoor spigot.
