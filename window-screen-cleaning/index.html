Window screen and track cleaning in Carroll County, GA

Clean glass behind a dirty screen still looks dull. Screens and tracks are part of every window cleaning we do.

Pricing

Screens

Spring pollen and summer dust clog screens fast in west Georgia. We wipe them down so light and air come through again.

Tracks and sills

Tracks collect dirt, dead bugs, and pollen. We clean them out so windows slide easily and water drains the way it should.

Price

There is no extra charge for screens and tracks. You pay by the pane: 20 ground-floor panes is $80.

Questions

Do you fix torn screens?

No. We clean them. Tell us about torn screens and we will handle them gently.

How often should screens be cleaned?

With your windows, twice a year: once in late spring after the pollen and once in fall.
