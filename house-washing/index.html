House washing in Carroll County, GA

Siding in west Georgia collects green algae, pollen film, and mildew on the shaded sides first. We wash houses in Carrollton and the towns around it, and we price each job after we see photos of your home.

Pricing
  • Priced per house after we see photos

What a house wash covers

We wash the siding, trim, and soffits, and rinse the windows and doors on the way. Soft washing uses lower pressure than a driveway job, so paint and vinyl are not stripped or dented.

How we price it

Houses differ a lot in height, shape, and how dirty they are, so there is no single rate. Text two or three photos and your address, and we send a price back for the whole house.

Pair it with the rest of the exterior

Most customers add the driveway at $0.15 per square foot, or the windows at $4 and $5 per pane. Doing it all in one visit saves a second trip.

Questions

Is house washing the same as pressure washing?

It starts from the same equipment, but we run lower pressure on siding so the surface is not damaged.

How often should siding be washed?

Many homes in our area look best washed every one to two years. A house with heavy tree cover can need it sooner.

Do I need to be home?

Usually not. We need access to outdoor water, and we confirm the plan with you by text.
