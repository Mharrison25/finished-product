Here are the questions we hear most. If yours is not here, text us.

Questions

What services do you offer?

Window cleaning, pressure washing, driveway cleaning, and trash can cleaning.

How do you price window cleaning?

By the pane. Ground floor panes are $4 and upper story panes are $5.

How do you price pressure washing and driveway cleaning?

By the square foot, at $0.15. Multiply length by width in feet.

How much is trash can cleaning?

$50 per can.

Where do you work?

Across Carroll County, including Carrollton, Villa Rica, Temple, Bremen, Bowdon, Mount Zion, Fairfield Plantation, Whitesburg, and Roopville. Text your address if you are not sure.

Which days do you work?

Most jobs run Tuesday through Saturday.

How do I get a quote?

Text or call (470) 248-0227, or email WestWashCo@gmail.com. Send your address and, for windows, your pane counts. For pressure washing, send photos and rough measurements.

Can you quote without visiting?

Often yes. Photos and measurements are usually enough.
