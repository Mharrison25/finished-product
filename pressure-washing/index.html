Pressure washing in Carroll County, GA

We pressure wash concrete and other hard surfaces around homes: driveways, front walks, patios, porch slabs, and steps. The rate is $0.15 per square foot, so you can check the math yourself.

Measure and multiply

Length times width gives square feet. Multiply by $0.15. A 20 by 30 foot driveway is 600 square feet, or $90. A 15 by 20 foot patio is 300 square feet, or $45.

Surfaces we wash

Concrete driveways, walkways, patios, porch slabs, and steps. For anything else, send a photo and ask. We say yes only when we can do the job without damaging the surface.
