About West Washing Company

West Washing Company is a two-person exterior cleaning business based in Carrollton, Georgia. We clean windows, pressure wash driveways and walks, and clean trash cans across Carroll County.

Who does the work

Mason does the on-site work. Hayden handles scheduling, quotes, and customer messages. When you text or call, you reach one of us.

How we price

Windows are $4 per ground floor pane and $5 per upstairs pane. Pressure washing and driveways are $0.15 per square foot. Trash cans are $50 each. You get a number before the work starts.

When we work

We work every day with flexible hours. Most jobs run Tuesday through Saturday.
