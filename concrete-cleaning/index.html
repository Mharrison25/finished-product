Concrete cleaning in Carroll County, GA

Georgia red clay, algae, and tire marks soak into concrete. We pressure wash driveways, slabs, walks, and steps for $0.15 per square foot.

Pricing
  • $0.15 per square foot

What it costs

Multiply length by width for each surface and add them up. A 600 square foot driveway is $90. A 600 square foot driveway plus a 100 square foot front walk is $105.

What comes off

Algae, mildew, red clay, tire marks, and most dirt streaks. Fresh oil often lightens a lot. Old oil and rust can stay faintly visible. We tell you what to expect before we start.

Even results

We use a surface cleaner on flat concrete, so it dries one even color instead of striped wand marks.

Questions

Send a photo and ask. We quote it by the square foot like any other slab.

Do I need to be home?

No, as long as we can reach an outdoor spigot.
